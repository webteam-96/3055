# Real photo sources — Rotary imagery (logo present, no club name)

> You asked for real photographs with the Rotary logo and **no club name** on them.
> I don't embed copyrighted photos into the published directory (licensing risk), so
> here are **properly-licensed sources** to download from yourself. Before placing any
> file in the printed book, confirm its licence allows print use and that **no other
> club's banner / name / crest is visible** in the frame.

## Best (official, licensed for Rotarian use)
- **Rotary Brand Center** — https://brandcenter.rotary.org  (Rotary member sign-in)
  - "Images" library: People of Action photos, the Masterbrand & Mark of Excellence
    (the wheel), campaign imagery. Free for official Rotary/district use.
  - "Logos" → download the Rotary wheel / Masterbrand in PNG/EPS/SVG (what these
    filler designs use).
- **Rotary International media / newsroom** — https://www.rotary.org/en/news-features
  and the official Rotary Flickr (https://www.flickr.com/photos/rotary) — many images
  are Rotary-owned; check the per-image usage note.

## Free-licence stock (generic — usually NO Rotary logo; add the wheel yourself)
- **Unsplash** — https://unsplash.com/s/photos/volunteer  (free licence)
- **Pexels** — https://www.pexels.com/search/community%20service/  (free licence)
- **Wikimedia Commons** — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rotary_International
  (has emblems/wheel; licence varies **per file** — read each one).

## Rules of thumb for picking
1. The **Rotary wheel** or Masterbrand should be visible, but **no individual club
   name/number** (district-level "Rotary District 3055" is fine).
2. Prefer high resolution: **≥ 2200 px** on the short edge for a full B5 print page.
3. Avoid dated event banners and recognizable faces unless you have consent.
4. Hand me any you download and I'll compose them into B5 / square filler pages that
   match these designs (logo lockup, caption, frame).
